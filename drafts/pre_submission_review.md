## R1 — Mechanism & novelty referee

**Summary.** The paper isolates "where selection happens" (before vs after the native 2×2 merger) as a design axis for merger-equipped VLMs, gives a three-part mechanism (M1 rank reshuffle, M2 anti-text demotion, M3 ranking-swap causal control), and derives a deliberately minimal method (RBM = query-blind L2 top-κ on merger-input units). The writing is honest to a fault and the negative results are genuinely valuable. My concerns are (i) the causal control is partly structural and its one cross-generation replication attempt *contradicts* the mechanism with no resolved root cause; (ii) the novelty positioning omits the most relevant merging literature (ToMe/AdaptMerge) and the "empty cell" claim is threatened by in-ViT pruning work the paper itself cites; (iii) several mechanism statements are imprecise about what the Qwen merger actually is.

**R1-1 [major]. The M3 swap≡pre "decisive test" is close to an identity, and the one diagnostic that would prove it is missing.** §4.3 rests on "unit equivalence": a kept unit's merged token is bit-identical at either stage. If that premise holds, swap≡pre follows *by construction* — it is a logical consequence, not an empirical discovery. The real empirical payload is then only M1/M2 (the rankings differ, and the difference is anti-text). Worse, the Qwen2.5-VL result (j3: swap 0.730 vs pre 0.538 at seq=1, +19.2 pp, 8/16 identity) shows the *same code pattern violates* unit equivalence on the second generation — which means the premise is empirically falsifiable and has been falsified once. The paper's stated root causes (window-attention `reverse_indices` misalignment, or batch-dependent merger) are exactly the artifacts that could, in mirror form, also produce a *false* swap≡pre on Qwen3. The decisive, cheap discriminator — did the swap path actually **keep the pre top-κ units**? — was never measured on *either* model (j3: "`--save-unit-scores` does not write kept indices"). **Action:** emit kept-unit indices in swap mode and report Jaccard(swap-kept, pre-top-κ) on both models. If ≈1.0 on Qwen3, the causal claim is genuinely hardened (the same units, via the same forward path, give the same accuracy — and on Qwen2.5 they evidently do not, pinpointing a real architectural difference to diagnose). Until then, M3 should be presented as "consistent with a pure-ranking effect under verified unit selection," not "the decisive causal proof." (~1–2 GPU·h + runner edit; j3 already scopes ~1 h.)

**R1-2 [major]. The causal decomposition is single-generation, single-scale, two-benchmark — and M1/M2 are not actually shown for Qwen2.5-VL.** M1–M3 tables (§4.1–4.3) are Qwen3-VL-8B only, n=64 (M1/M2) and n=200 (M3), on TextVQA+DocVQA. The cross-generation "corroboration of the ranking law" (§4.5) claims M1 for Qwen2.5-VL, but the paper prints **no** Qwen2.5-VL ρ/τ/Jaccard (M1) or M2 numbers — j3 measured only the stage law (L2 pre>post), the (failed) swap, and selector invariance. Either (a) run the deterministic M1/M2 capture on Qwen2.5-VL (CPU analysis of existing capture mechanism; cheap) and add the rows, or (b) explicitly state that M1/M2 are Qwen3-only and Qwen2.5 corroboration is the stage-law accuracy pattern + VZ≡post only. As written, §4.5's "M1 + stage-law + VZ≡post" overstates what is tabulated.

**R1-3 [major]. Related work omits ToMe and AdaptMerge — the nearest literature to the paper's own thesis — and the "empty cell" claim must be delimited against in-ViT pruning.** Token Merging (ToMe, Bolya et al., ICLR'23) is the origin of token *merging* and is not cited, yet the paper's central object is a merger and PruMerge (a ToMe-descendant) *is* cited. More damaging: the project's own survey (notes/lit-survey.md rows 38–39, 166–167) identifies **AdaptMerge** (ToMe→LLaVA, EMNLP'25 Findings) as "nearest 'ToMe for LLaVA'; query-conditioned merging … closes most of the OCR gap vs plain ToMe" — a merging-based VLM-compression method that explicitly addresses merger-induced OCR degradation, i.e. the paper's headline phenomenon, from the merging side. A novelty referee will find this omission within minutes. Separately, the §2 "empty cell" claim ("no published work selects tokens before a VLM's native merger") is threatened by the paper's *own* citation of **QuietPrune** (query-guided *early in-ViT* pruning): pruning ViT tokens upstream of the merger is, topologically, pre-merger selection. **Action:** add a merging-lineage paragraph (ToMe → PruMerge → AdaptMerge) with an explicit relation sentence (they merge/prune *within or after* the encoder with *trained or attention* scorers; none isolates the *stage* variable under an iso-selector control on a *native* merger; AdaptMerge's OCR-gap finding is corroborating evidence for the lossy-merger thesis, cited not competed); and restate the empty cell as "no work isolates the pre-vs-post-merger *stage* as the experimental variable under iso-model/iso-token/iso-selector control" — defensible, unlike the literal topological claim.

**R1-4 [major→framing]. The *method* contribution is thin; the paper half-claims it.** RBM = L2 top-κ before the merger — arguably the simplest conceivable method, and the paper's own §6 shows no decoration works. That is fine — the contribution is the *finding + mechanism + stage axis* — but Contribution 2 ("A method (RBM) and its cross-generation law") invites a "novelty of the method" attack the paper cannot win. **Action:** reframe Contribution 2 as "a stage law" (the empirical result) and state in §3.2, once, that the method's minimalism is a deliberate *control design* (the simplest query-blind scorer isolates stage), with the practical prescription following from §6. Pre-empts the most likely "incremental method" objection by owning it.

**R1-5 [major-minor]. "Lossy 2×2 pooling/averaging" mischaracterizes the Qwen PatchMerger, and one mechanism sentence is wrong at unit granularity.** Qwen-VL's PatchMerger is a learned MLP projection over the *concatenated* four patch features (plus deepstack mergers), not an average/pool; the abstract and title-3 say "lossy 2×2 pooling," and §3.3 claims selection protects glyph patches "from being averaged with surrounding background *before* the keep decision." At unit granularity, selection cannot prevent intra-unit combination — it prevents whole units from being *dropped on the basis of corrupted post-merger saliency*. **Action:** (i) describe the merger accurately (concat + MLP projection; dimensionality 4→1 is the lossy step; state whether it is literally an MLP in §3.1); (ii) rewrite §3.3's protection sentence accordingly; (iii) title candidate 3's "pooling" → "merger." A mechanism paper must be exact about the mechanism's object.

**R1-6 [minor]. No hypothesis for *why* the learned merger demotes text.** M2 documents the anti-text direction (DocVQA ρ=+0.44, group (a) Sobel 0.64 vs 0.12) but never explains it. A one-sentence, flagged-as-speculative account — the merger is trained for visual-language alignment on general content, and the 4→1 projection attenuates high-frequency stroke energy so merged text units score low under any post-merger norm — would complete the mechanism narrative and is testable (compare L2 vs a high-frequency-weighted post scorer). Optional but high-value for a mechanism-section referee.

**R1-7 [minor]. §4.1's prose contradicts its own table on the text-density gradient.** Text: "reshuffle strongest on DocVQA and weakest on object-centric GQA." Table: ρ = 0.137 (DocVQA) / 0.332 (TextVQA) / **0.360 (GQA)** — GQA has the *largest* full-ranking correlation (least reshuffle) and the *largest* Jaccard@25% (0.278), so the claim is only true as "DocVQA worst"; between TextVQA and GQA, ρ actually *increases* toward object-centric while the *accuracy* effect vanishes. The honest, stronger statement: the reshuffle is ubiquitous, but its **directionality** (M2 ρ: 0.44/0.16/0.04) is what tracks text density and accuracy. Fix the sentence — a careful referee cross-checks table vs prose, and this one currently fails.

**R1-8 [minor]. "The community treats the merger as lossless" is near-strawman.** The paper's *own* strongest external anchor — the VisionZip authors' README caveat that gains are "less striking" on Qwen2.5-VL because the merger already compresses — shows practitioners knew the merger matters. §1's "implicitly treated … as lossless" should be softened to "the stage of selection has not been isolated as an experimental variable; the debate ran only over scorer and budget." Same claim, defensible.

**R1-9 [minor]. deepstack-merger interaction under-explained.** RBM applies the pre-mask to *all* deepstack mergers (Algorithm 1, lines 5–6). A referee will ask whether deepstack tokens (intermediate-depth features of the *same* units) leak pruned-unit information back, and whether masking them is a choice or forced. One sentence in §3.3: unit identity is shared across main+deepstack streams, so pruning a unit removes it from every stream; state this is the architectural contract, not a hyperparameter. (No new experiment needed; pre-empts a question.)

**R1-10 [minor]. M2's text proxy is single (Sobel) and validated on few samples.** ρ(rank_shift, edge) is moderate (0.44) and the "92% vs 35% above median" contrast carries the claim. A second, independent text-stroke proxy (Laplacian variance, or MSER stroke width) recomputed offline on the existing capture would convert "proxy" into "robust to proxy choice." CPU-only; optional but cheap insurance for the DocVQA mechanism paragraph.

**R1 verdict.** Mechanism story is the paper's crown jewel and, with R1-1/R1-2/R1-5 fixed, publishable-strength. The causal claim must be downgraded or *verified at the unit-set level* (R1-1) — the Qwen2.5 contradiction makes the current "decisive" language untenable as written. Novelty survives but requires the ToMe/AdaptMerge engagement and the delimited empty-cell claim (R1-3). **Recommendation: major revision.**

## R2 — Experimental rigor referee

**Summary.** The evaluation is, for a single-A40 study, unusually complete: full official splits for the proposed method (26/26 cells), official scorers with ground-truth self-test, pre-registered gates, and extensive configuration disclosure. My concerns are statistical scope-matching of the cross-method comparisons, two missing/ambiguous cells, and a possible method-description vs implementation mismatch in the scoring feature tap.

**R2-1 [major]. The headline cross-method comparisons are cross-scope (n=500 baselines vs full-split ours), and the flagship "FastV wins" cell is not significant as reported.** Table 1's FastV/Pyramid columns are n=500 (footnote ʰ) against full-split RBM/post; §5.3 honestly computes pooled z, but the result is that **Qwen3-VL TextVQA, FastV > RBM (+4.1 pp), is direction-only (z = 1.79)** — yet the Abstract, §1 and §8 state "FastV wins the query-relevant benchmarks" with this cell as a pillar. The defensible pillars are Qwen2.5-VL TextVQA (z=2.56), Qwen3-VL GQA (z=2.69) and same-pixel DocVQA (n=200). **Action:** for the two benchmarks with *no* pixel-cap obstruction (TextVQA, GQA), run FastV at full split (or n ≥ 2000, both models) in the validated HF harness so the decisive cross-method contrasts are same-scope; this is serially feasible on one A40 (HF eager, no attention-materialization blowup on these two) and converts the paper's most-attacked contrast from "direction-only" to a verdict either way. If budget forbids, reword the abstract to "FastV wins significantly on Qwen2.5-VL TextVQA and Qwen3-VL GQA; the Qwen3-VL TextVQA lead is direction-only at current n."

**R2-2 [major]. Table 1 has a blank anchor cell (Qwen3-VL none-DocVQA) on a headline benchmark.** Footnote ᵃ explains (context overrun at native resolution; pre/post run under the same config, compared to each other). Legitimate, but a main table with a missing uncompressed anchor invites "how much is retained?" skepticism, and the fix exists within constraints: the n=200 600k-pixel Qwen3 none cell (0.951) is already measured (j4d). **Action:** add an explicitly-labeled capped-resolution anchor row (Qwen3 none-DocVQA @600k, full split or n≥2000, ~0.95) next to the matched capped pre/post cells, so readers see absolute retention; keep native-resolution pre-vs-post as the headline contrast. This costs one capped run and closes the visual hole.

**R2-3 [major]. Scoring-feature tap: method description and implementation appear to diverge.** §3.1/Algorithm 1 score units on "merger-*input* features," which reads as final-ViT-layer features fed to the main merger. The mechanism report (`mechanism_verification_report` §0) states PRE scores are "block-8 **deepstack[0]-input** unit L2" — an *intermediate-depth* ViT feature, not the main merger's input. If the deployed method scores at block 8, then (i) the method description is inaccurate, and (ii) the choice of feature depth is an unreported hyperparameter that could matter. **Action:** state the exact feature tap in §3.3 (and that it is identical for pre and post *after* the corresponding merger stage, preserving the control); and run one 1-cell sensitivity (block-8 vs final-layer features, TextVQA Qwen3 @25%, n=200). If inert, one sentence closes it; if not, disclose as a design choice with numbers. (Cross-listed with R1-1; both are cheap.)

**R2-4 [major-minor]. The smallest text-dense Δ is configuration-conditional, with a sign reversal disclosed only in a footnote.** J8b: Qwen2.5-VL DocVQA flips from **+11.0 pp pre lead (native full split)** to **+8.0 pp post lead at the 600k cap** (0.424 vs 0.504). This means "pre-merger wins all text-dense benchmarks" is *not* true for the weakest cell under the very cap the baseline comparison requires. The disclosure exists (footnote ⁱ, §5.4) and the reading (shallow-regime limit, same root cause as the GQA sign and the 75%-retention ties) is coherent — but a 20-line footnote is the wrong home for a sign reversal of a headline claim. **Action:** promote to a short paragraph in §5.2 ("Boundary: the stage effect is resolution-conditional; at low pixel budgets the sign reverses on the milder Qwen2.5-VL merger") and ensure no headline sentence quantifies text-dense wins in a way that silently relies on that cell. The full-split native range (+11.0 to +38.4) remains fine as the headline once labeled native-resolution.

**R2-5 [minor]. Use the paired test as the *primary* full-split test.** Pre and post are evaluated on identical images/questions (same split, greedy). Table 1's Δ₂₅ uses independent-binomial z; McNemar is reported only for GQA (8.1/7.1). Compute per-sample paired z (McNemar for exact-match; paired-difference SE for VQA-acc/ANLS item scores) for all six text-dense Δ cells — offline, CPU-only on existing per-sample JSON. They will be ≥ the independent z; reporting them as primary is statistically correct and removes the "independent-sample assumption" attack surface. Relatedly, footnote ᵈ's Bernoulli-rate approximation for ANLS/OCR-Bench is defensible but not guaranteed conservative (ANLS has partial-credit items); report the empirical per-item SD alongside, or label the approximation explicitly as "rate approximation; item-score SD = …".

**R2-6 [minor]. FastV layer-K=2 is asserted, not justified or stress-tested.** FastV @25% drops by LLM layer-2 attention (footnote ʰ). A baseline-tuning referee will ask whether K was chosen to weaken or strengthen the competitor. **Action:** cite FastV's own layer-2 default (their attention-negligibility observation) and add a tiny sensitivity sweep K ∈ {1, 3, 4} at n=200 (TextVQA+GQA, one model) to the appendix. Cheap in the HF harness; pre-empts "you tuned the baseline to lose OCR" (the OCR collapse is the paper's key contrast, so its robustness to K matters).

**R2-7 [minor]. Engine-equivalence evidence is thinner than the fairness protocol implies.** The r=0 anchor is 8/8 per-sample identical and HF-vs-vLLM none is "16/16 equivalent" (j4_step2_fix). For a cross-engine accuracy comparison this is the load-bearing validation. **Action:** appendix table of the 16 none cells (model × benchmark × engine: metric value, |Δ|, per-sample agreement rate on a shared subset). If HF and vLLM none agree to ≤1 pp at n=500, say so numerically; "16/16 equivalent" without the tolerance is not checkable.

**R2-8 [minor]. Skip accounting should be a table, and its conservatism direction stated per contrast.** OCR-Bench none cells skip 18/24 of 1000 (scored 0, conservative for none); compressed cells skip ≤5; HF OCR cells skip ≤1 of 500. **Action:** appendix table of skips per method × benchmark × model; and state the conservatism direction for each cross-method contrast (e.g., if RBM skips ≥ FastV on OCR n=500, the +25.9 pp RBM lead is conservative — worth one explicit sentence, as it is the paper's single strongest cross-method win).

**R2-9 [minor]. Ablation completeness: mostly good; two gaps.** Budget curve (3 points × 2 models × 2 benches) ✓; selector invariance (2 families; honest Qwen2.5 attenuation) ✓; granularity-as-definition ✓ (defensible); efficiency (stage-neutral, single engine) ✓. Gaps: (a) no ablation of *which merger stream* the mask applies to (main only vs main+deepstack; see R1-9 — a 2-cell run settles it); (b) the retention curve is L2-only at 75/50/25% — the selector-invariance table is 25%-only; extending the attn family to 50% on Qwen3 (2 cells) would show invariance across the curve, not one point. Both cheap; (a) is the higher priority.

**R2-10 [minor]. Efficiency scope is narrow for a methods paper (disclosed, but flag the risk).** Table 3 = one model (Qwen3), one benchmark (TextVQA n=200), one engine (vLLM offline), no serving/continuous-batching, no memory measurement, latency at n=1 ×5. The stage-neutral finding and +68%/−36% numbers are sound and honestly bounded. For ICME specifically, efficiency is a fit criterion: if any budget remains, one TTFT-at-concurrency (c=16) measurement and the Qwen2.5 row would materially strengthen §5.7. Not required for the claim as scoped.

**R2-11 [minor]. Greedy decoding ⇒ binomial SE only; acceptable, one addition.** Disclosed (§7.6) and standard. Add a one-line reproducibility statement (seed=0, greedy, code + scorers + per-sample JSON release at github.com/Code-Yan-ZX/VLA) near §5.1 — the paper's strongest rigor asset (official per-sample rescore chain) should be advertised, not only implied.

**R2-12 [minor]. GQA flip decomposition.** McNemar agreement is 84.5%/83.5% with net +2.8/+2.6 pp post. Reporting the two flip counts (pre→post vs post→pre) would show the net is a small imbalance of a large two-way churn — consistent with the "near-null ranking, small post edge" mechanism (§4.4). One clause; strengthens the honest-GQA narrative.

**R2 verdict.** Statistical foundations are solid and the disclosure culture is exemplary; the fixable issues are scope-matching of cross-method contrasts (R2-1), the blank anchor (R2-2), the feature-tap ambiguity (R2-3), and promoting the sign-reversal disclosure (R2-4). None overturns a claim; all are actionable within the stated compute. **Recommendation: minor-to-major revision** (major only if R2-1/R2-3 are left unaddressed).

Move all z/ratio detail to §5.

**R3-5 [minor]. ## Meta-review (synthesis & decision guidance)

**Paper profile.** A finding-driven methods paper whose real contributions are (1) the isolation of the selection *stage* as a first-class axis for merger-equipped VLMs, (2) a causal-ish mechanism account (M1–M3) of lossy-merger ranking corruption, (3) an unusually fair same-model baseline program, and (4) a pre-registered negative-result closure. The method itself is deliberately trivial. This profile fits **Pattern Recognition** (empirical-law + mechanism + controlled large-scale evaluation) and **ICME** (multimedia + efficiency table) well; it is not a "new architecture beats SOTA" paper and must not be reviewed as one.

**Cross-reviewer convergences (the 3 issues all referees touch):**
1. *The causal claim's status* (R1-1/R1-2 × R2-3): swap≡pre is near-structural, failed on Qwen2.5, was never verified at the kept-unit level, and the scoring feature tap is ambiguously described. One coordinated fix-package (kept-set Jaccard diagnostic + feature-tap specification/1-cell ablation, ~2–3 GPU·h) resolves all three.
2. *Cross-scope/cross-config baselines* (R2-1/R2-2/R2-4 × R3-4/R3-7): the n=500-vs-full-split scope gap, the blank Qwen3 none-DocVQA anchor, and the 600k sign reversal are individually disclosed but collectively soften the headline. Same-scope FastV on TextVQA/GQA + a capped anchor row + abstract-range correction closes it.
